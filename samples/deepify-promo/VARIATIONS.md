# Variation Menu

Ten template concepts for Deepify book promos. The first three are built; the remaining seven are ready to add on request. All run at 1080×1920 portrait, 15 seconds, with the locked Deepify palette.

## Built

### 01 · Hero Cover — "the cover IS the story"

Cover drops in at ~0.4s as the visual anchor. Kicker ("Deepify Academy · Book X") above, author name below. Tagline scene, benefits scene, CTA.

**Best for:** generic launch promo, reels that need to show the product clearly.

### 02 · Kinetic Type — "the title is the hero"

No cover for the first ~10 seconds. The book title slams in word-by-word with varied easing and scale. Cover revealed at ~10s as the payoff, CTA closes.

**Best for:** punchy social hooks, videos where stopping-the-scroll matters more than product clarity.

### 03 · Problem → Solution — "do you feel this pain?"

Opens with three pain statements ("Buried in notes?" / "Can't find anything?" / "Losing your best ideas?"). Cover lands at ~6s as the answer. Three-beat promise, CTA.

**Best for:** audiences who don't know they need the book yet. Strongest for cold traffic / ad creative.

## Available on request

### 04 · Stat Hook — "90% of your ideas are lost"

Open with a single eye-catching stat animated as a counter (0 → 90%). Cover appears as the fix, three-beat promise, CTA.

**Needs:** one concrete stat per book. Make something up or cite a source — for AI books, citations like "Forrester" / "IDC" work well.

### 05 · Question + Promise — "what if your brain remembered everything?"

Opens on a question in italic serif. Cover appears as the answer. Three promise beats ("You'd capture X. / You'd find Y. / You'd think Z.") with the word that matters highlighted in Deepify blue. CTA.

**Best for:** aspirational positioning. Feels more "TED-talky" than sales-y.

### 06 · Logo Stinger — "brand-forward bumper"

Deepify logo animates in (circuit brain / wordmark reveal), ~1.5s bumper. Cover flash-cuts in. Book title + CTA. Shortest, most "channel trailer" feel.

**Best for:** series intros, YouTube channel trailers, Instagram story ad bumpers.

### 07 · Series Spread — "the whole academy"

All four books fan out like a bookshelf at 0-3s, featured book zooms forward at 3-6s, then continues as a standard hero cover promo. Good for "explore the academy" messaging.

**Best for:** top-of-funnel discovery. Use once you have 4+ books on the shelf.

### 08 · Pages Flying — "peek inside"

Cover lands briefly at 0-2s, then "opens" — three chapter titles fly through the frame as pages (with real page numbers / chapter names). Cover closes at ~11s, CTA closes.

**Best for:** making the book feel substantive. Works best when you have strong chapter titles.

### 09 · Testimonial — "five stars"

Pull quote animates in as if handwritten ("This book changed how I take notes."). Five-star rating counts up. Cover appears, CTA closes.

**Needs:** real testimonial text + star rating. If no reviews yet, skip this template until you have them.

### 10 · Motion Graphic — "book-specific visualization"

Each book gets a tailored visual that animates on-brand:

- `book-3a-second-brain` — neurons/synapses firing, information flowing into a central brain
- `book-3b-second-brain-201` — notes as connected graph nodes, edges lighting up one by one
- `book-voice-agent` — audio waveform responding to an implicit voice, agent "listens" and "replies"
- `book-local-ai` — a server rack with GPUs lighting up, data flowing through circuits

Cover appears at the climax (~9s) as the answer to the visualization. Most impressive, most effort.

**Best for:** hero launch, pinned tweet, one-screen-explainer video.
