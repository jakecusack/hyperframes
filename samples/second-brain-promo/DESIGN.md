# AI Second Brain Promo — Design

Branding matched to the _Build Your AI Second Brain_ cover and the Deepify Academy identity. Swiss-Pulse-adjacent: clinical, precise, confident. The book is about thinking clearly with AI, so the video should feel technical but human.

## Style Prompt

Deep navy canvas with a single sky-blue accent. Type is strong uppercase sans for headlines, regular-weight sans for body. Hard, purposeful entrances on `power3.out` and `expo.out`. The cover is the visual anchor — it drops in first and nothing competes with it. One accent-colored word per frame, maximum.

## Colors

- `#2C4764` — canvas deep (top of navy gradient)
- `#3A5F87` — canvas lift (bottom of navy gradient)
- `#FFFFFF` — primary text
- `#3B9FDE` — Deepify blue (single accent: the word that matters, underlines, CTA)
- `#AFBCC7` — muted slate (secondary text, metadata, dividers)

## Typography

- Headlines: **Inter Tight** Bold, uppercase, slight negative tracking
- Body / bullets: **Inter** 400–500, sentence case
- Metadata / labels: **Inter** 500, uppercase, wide tracking (`0.24em`)

## Motion

- Easing: `power3.out`, `expo.out`, `power2.out` — snap-and-settle, not drift
- Durations: 0.5–0.8s for entrances, short holds of 0.4–1.0s between beats
- Scene transitions: cross-fade with 0.5s overlap. No shader glitches.

## What NOT to Do

- No warm earth tones — stay in the navy/blue family
- No serif type — this book is technical, not literary
- No more than one `#3B9FDE` element per frame
- No `back.out`/`elastic` — undermines the precision
- Don't stretch the cover — maintain 2:3 aspect ratio
